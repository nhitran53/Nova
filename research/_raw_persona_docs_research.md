# Raw research: Nova persona LoRA + grounded repair docs (Qwen3.5-4B)
Compiled 2026-10-24. Web-only. Dates are source/creation/merge dates where captured; "undated" = page fetched today without a visible date. Labels: **[D]** direct evidence from fetched page, **[I]** my inference, **UNVERIFIED** = not confirmed from a fetched source.

## 1. Qwen3.5-4B architecture, tooling, hardware

- **[D] Architecture.** Unified vision-language model (early-fusion multimodal), "Efficient Hybrid Architecture: Gated Delta Networks", 201 languages. HF card https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B (undated).
- **[D] Layer detail.** 24 GatedDeltaNet (linear-attention) layers + 8 full-attention layers (1 full per 3 linear), mrope, 262k ctx, 27-layer vision tower; hub default class is `Qwen3_5ForConditionalGeneration`; text-only `Qwen3_5ForCausalLM` avoids loading the vision tower. Source: https://aiml.training/docs/tutorials/lora-fine-tuning-qwen3.5-4b/ (verified run 2026-10-19). Third-party https://ayushdeva.com/qwen35-architecture.html says 32 layers / 2560 wide (snippet only, undated).
- **[D] LoRA target modules.** `q/k/v/o_proj` exist only in the 8 full-attention layers; `in_proj_*`/`out_proj` only in the 24 GDN layers; MLP projections everywhere (same aiml.training page, 2026-10-19).
- **[D] Unsloth.** Supports Qwen3.5 0.8B–122B incl. 4B; bf16 LoRA VRAM: 4B ≈ 10 GB (0.8B 3, 2B 5, 9B 22, 27B 56). Requires transformers v5. **"It is not recommended to do QLoRA (4-bit) training on the Qwen3.5 models, no matter MoE or dense, due to higher than normal quantization differences."** Custom Mamba/Triton kernels make first run slow to compile. https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.5/fine-tune (undated; contains Apr-2026 screenshots, Mar-2026 updates).
- **[D] Unsloth Windows.** Unsloth Studio installs natively on Windows via PowerShell (`irm https://unsloth.ai/install.ps1 | iex`), also macOS/Linux/WSL (same page). Windows library install guide https://unsloth.ai/docs/get-started/install/windows-installation was fetched but not read in detail → requirements UNVERIFIED.
- **[D] Real QLoRA numbers (NOT Unsloth, plain PEFT+TRL, NF4, text-only head, no flash kernels).** RTX 3080 Ti 12 GB, Linux, torch 2.13.0+cu130, transformers 5.19.0, peft 0.21.2, bitsandbytes 0.50.2, trl 1.16.0: 500 Dolly examples × 3 epochs, seq 512, bs1×GA16, r=16/α=32, LoRA on 12 targets (21.2M params) → **279 s, 1367 tok/s, peak training VRAM 6.70 GB (7.35 GB nvidia-smi reserved)**. 9B NF4 load alone 7.92 GB and only ~192 tok seq fits in 12 GB. https://aiml.training/docs/tutorials/lora-fine-tuning-qwen3.5-4b/ (2026-10-19).
- **[I] 8 GB RTX 5070 Laptop:** 6.70 GB peak at seq 512 leaves ~1.3 GB headroom on paper, but Windows desktop/display and driver overhead typically take 0.5–1 GB of dedicated VRAM on a hybrid-graphics laptop; seq 1024 is probably tight (activations grow; gradient checkpointing helps). Treat as "fits at 512, risky at 1024" until measured. Throughput scales with GPU: 5070 Laptop is a different class than 3080 Ti; ~1–2k examples × ~300 tok × 3 epochs ≈ 1–2M tokens ≈ **roughly 10–40 min** at ~1k tok/s (extrapolation, UNVERIFIED on 5070 Laptop).
- **[D] Qwen3-1.7B/4B (not 3.5) on 8 GB:** techwithsergiu/qwen-qlora-train, Unsloth QLoRA validated on RTX 3070 8 GB for Qwen3 1.7B/4B; 8B OOMs. Has Qwen3.5 configs, but 8 GB Qwen3.5-4B validation not confirmed. https://github.com/techwithsergiu/qwen-qlora-train (undated). Note this conflicts with Unsloth's own anti-4-bit advice for Qwen3.5.
- **[D] Blackwell/Windows (sm_120).** Windows RTX 5070 Ti laptop works with Unsloth after `TORCH_CUDA_ARCH_LIST=12.0`, fixing hybrid-graphics GPU preference, fixing CUDA DLL PATH conflicts (torch 2.11.0+cu130, driver 595.79) — https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth/issues/5266 (2026-05-04). Unsloth maintainer pointed to docker (sm_120-tested). bitsandbytes: maintainer says sm_120 compiled on Windows+Linux since ~v0.45.3; a "no kernel image" report was in issue https://github.com/bitsandbytes-foundation/bitsandbytes/issues/1937 (2026-05-05, closed 2026-05-26; reporter used Python 3.14 – root cause UNVERIFIED). **[I]** Use cu128/cu130 torch wheels; WSL2/Linux avoids most DLL pain but needs NVIDIA WSL driver; native Windows is workable but fiddly.
- **[D] mlx-lm on Qwen3.5 (Apple):** works but slow. GatedDeltaNet uses `use_kernel=not self.training` → Python-loop fallback in training (Metal kernel has no VJP). Measured on **M4 Pro 24 GB**, mlx 0.32.2, mlx-lm 0.31.3, Qwen3.5-4B-Base 4-bit, ~600 tok/example, bs1, grad-ckpt, mask-prompt, max-seq 1024: `--num-layers 16` 0.073 it/s, 14.8 GB; 8 layers 0.139 it/s, 14.8 GB; 5 layers 0.195 it/s, 12.6 GB. Issue OPEN as of fetch. https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-lm/issues/1956 (2026-10-08). **[I]** 1k examples × 2 epochs at bs1 ≈ 2000 iters ≈ 3 h (16 layers) to ~3 h/… (0.139→~4 h; 0.195→~2.8 h); roughly "hours". No M5 Pro numbers found → UNVERIFIED (M5 may be faster but the bottleneck is the Python loop). mlx_lm.lora: QLoRA automatic if model is quantized; `--mask-prompt` for completion-only loss; chat/completions/text jsonl formats; `--grad-checkpoint`, `--num-layers`, `--batch-size` for memory. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ml-explore/mlx-lm/main/mlx_lm/LORA.md (undated).
- **Recommendation [I]:** Prefer the **Windows/WSL2 RTX 5070 laptop** (CUDA, ~5–20× faster than the mlx GDN fallback) using plain PEFT+TRL NF4 (proven 6.7 GB) or Unsloth bf16 LoRA if it fits (4B bf16 ≈ 10 GB → **does not fit 8 GB**; Unsloth also discourages 4-bit for this model). Fallback: train 2B or 0.8B Qwen3.5 in bf16, or use Mac with fewer layers and be patient. Alternative: rent a free/cheap Colab T4/L4 with the Unsloth 4B notebook (free notebooks exist per Unsloth page). Mac 24 GB is a viable safety net (12–15 GB used, but slow).

## 2. Persona/style SFT recipe

- **Chat template & thinking [D].** Official template (https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B/raw/main/chat_template.jinja, undated): generation prompt appends `<|im_start|>assistant\n<think>\n\n</think>\n\n` when `enable_thinking is false`, otherwise `<think>\n`. For assistant turns after the last user query, history is rendered as `<think>\n{reasoning}\n</think>\n\n{content}`; with empty reasoning this yields `<think>\n\n</think>\n\n{content}` — **[I] i.e. training examples rendered through this template with no reasoning exactly match the inference prompt with enable_thinking=false.** The template has **no /think or /no_think soft switch** (absent from template text). Earlier-turn assistant messages drop the think block (history is stripped).
- **[D] TRL support.** TRL PR #5824 (merged 2026-05-25) adds Qwen3.5 Think/NoThink training templates with `{% generation %}` markers for assistant-only loss (`assistant_only_loss=True`); https://github.com/huggingface/trl/pull/5824. File naming hints 4B+ → think template, ≤2B → nothink template (**[I]** from filenames; check which is auto-applied for 4B).
- **[D] Unsloth advice on reasoning:** to preserve reasoning, keep ≥75% reasoning examples; "otherwise you can emit it fully" (fine-tune page). So a no-think-only LoRA is explicitly accepted.
- **Dataset size/format.** LIMA (Zhou et al., arXiv 2305.11206): ~1,000 curated examples suffice for style/format alignment — **not fetched this session, UNVERIFIED here; cited from memory**. For Nova: 800–2,000 short chat examples (system prompt + user/tool-result facts → 1–3 sentence reply), JSONL `messages` format (mlx-lm "chat" format and TRL both accept it).
- **Hyperparameters [I]/example [D].** The verified run used r=16, α=32, dropout 0.05, lr 2e-4, 3 epochs, seq 512, eff batch 16 (aiml.training, 2026-10-19). Unsloth sample uses r=16, α=16, dropout 0. **[I]** For style-only: r=8–16, α=r–2r, lr 1e-4–2e-4 (cosine, warmup 3%), 2–3 epochs, hold out 10% for val loss; stop when val loss flattens (over-training increases number/forgetting risk). Sampling for deployment (Unsloth, non-thinking general): temp 0.7, top_p 0.8, top_k 20, presence_penalty 1.5 — **[I]** for monotone persona consider temp 0.3–0.5 and verify presence_penalty doesn't hurt repeated numbers (penalty may discourage repeating a number token!). https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.5
- **Synthetic data [I]:** generate with a larger model from templates: given structured "facts" (tool JSON) + persona rules, produce paraphrases; filter programmatically (numbers in output ⊆ numbers in facts, length, banned phrases, no emojis); human-review ~100.
- **Preventing number corruption [I]:** (a) train on prompts that include the exact number strings and targets that copy them verbatim, with diverse formats (decimals, units, ranges, times); (b) keep loss on assistant tokens only; (c) optionally use placeholders (`{speed_kmh}`) in training and fill deterministically (safest—LLM never emits digits); (d) keep verifier gate as final authority; (e) include negatives where facts lack a number and reply says so. No source fetched — all design inference.
- **Evals [I]:** held-out prompts, style judge/regex (sentence count, no filler), number-fidelity exact-match rate (see §4), regression set.

## 3. Fine-tune vs RAG for manuals

- **[D] Ovadia et al.**: unsupervised fine-tuning gives some improvement but **RAG consistently outperforms it for both existing and new knowledge**; LLMs struggle to learn new facts via fine-tuning; many paraphrases of a fact help. arXiv 2312.05934 (v3 2024-01-30), EMNLP 2024 https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.15/ ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.05934
- **[D] Gekhman et al.**: new knowledge learned slower than known; once learned, it **linearly increases hallucination**; fine-tuning mostly teaches use of pre-existing knowledge. arXiv 2405.05904 (v3 2024-10-01), EMNLP 2024 https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05904
- **[I]** Conclusion for Nova: use LoRA for persona/style only; keep manual content out of weights; use retrieval + "answer only from passages" prompt + refusal when retrieval score is low. Safety procedures (torque, voltage, jacking) must be copied verbatim from retrieved steps and numbers pass the existing verifier against the retrieved text.
- **Chunking [I]:** one chunk = one whole procedure (title + warnings + numbered steps), never split mid-list; prepend section path/title and WARNING/CAUTION blocks to each chunk; store step numbers; for long procedures, chunk by step-groups but repeat heading + warnings; return the whole procedure when top chunk is a part of it. Example open-source procedural RAG: https://github.com/ereztdev/RAGOps (deterministic troubleshooting RAG that refuses when docs don't support; README, undated — design reference only, not evaluated).
- **Hybrid retrieval [I]:** BM25 + dense with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, optional cross-encoder rerank (example hybrid RRF repo: https://github.com/saftyy/hybrid-rag-enterprise-support, undated, unevaluated). For a tiny fake manual (<200 chunks), BM25 alone + a score threshold is probably enough.
- **Embedding models [D]:** Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B: 28 layers, 32K ctx, 1024-dim (MRL 32–1024), instruction-aware, GGUF q8_0/f16 official https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B-GGUF and https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-Embedding (undated). Apache-2.0 per third-party note (https://github.com/SirWhack/context-kernel/blob/main/docs/research/S0_embed.md; confirm on model card—UNVERIFIED first-party). bge/nomic specifics: not researched → UNVERIFIED.
- **Stdlib-only BM25 [I]:** ~40 lines: tokenise `re.findall(r"[a-z0-9]+")`, Okapi BM25 k1=1.2, b=0.75, IDF `ln(1+(N-n+0.5)/(n+0.5))`. Existing libs: rank_bm25 (Apache-2.0) https://github.com/dorianbrown/rank_bm25 ; bm25s (MIT, numpy/scipy) https://github.com/xhluca/bm25s — both non-stdlib.
- **Small-model reasoning over retrieved text:** no benchmark fetched for 4B → UNVERIFIED. **[I]** keep 1–3 passages, ≤1.5k tokens, ask for extractive step reading; optionally have router (not the LLM) return the procedure steps and LLM only wraps wording.

## 4. Evaluating groundedness / refusal / forgetting (all design **[I]**; no sources fetched)

- **Test set (~60–100 Qs):** 40% answerable-with-exact-gold-steps; 20% answerable-by-paraphrase/synonym; 25% unanswerable (topic absent, part number not in manual, adjacent-topic distractor); 10% safety-critical (numbers, warnings); 5% conflicting/multi-hop.
- **Metrics:** retrieval hit@k (gold chunk id); refusal precision/recall on unanswerables (exact phrase "I don't have that procedure" or regex); citation check (cited chunk ids ⊆ retrieved ids, and each step sentence has ≥ fuzzy-match to a source step); step-order and step-count match; every number/unit in the answer appears in retrieved text (reuse number verifier); LLM-judge optional with larger model.
- **Abstention tests:** run with the gold chunk removed (must refuse); with misleading distractor retrieved (must refuse or say conflict).
- **Forgetting:** own 30–50 prompt regression set (arithmetic, tool-wording, instruction following) compared base vs LoRA; optionally a small slice (e.g. 100–200 items) of IFEval and MMLU via lm-evaluation-harness — harness/tool specifics not fetched → UNVERIFIED.
- **Number fidelity:** template-generated 200 cases with random values/units; require digit-exact echo; track rate pre/post LoRA.

## 5. Export paths

- **llama.cpp LoRA→GGUF:** `convert_lora_to_gguf.py` crashed on Qwen3.5 adapters (`_reorder_v_heads` → `LoraTorchTensor.reshape` NotImplementedError; issue #21125 opened 2026-03-28) until PR #28324 merged **2026-09-30**. https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/21125 ; https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/28324. **[I]** Use llama.cpp build after 2026-09-30. Issue's adapter targeted all q/k/v/o/gate/up/down/out_proj, r=32 — conversions of such targets are what the fix verified (rank-32 matrices matched to 9B dims per PR). `in_proj_*` GDN targets conversion: UNVERIFIED.
- **Adapter at runtime [D-ish, issue questions]:** llama-server `--lora adapter.gguf` is the deployment pattern in the issue; adapter on top of a *quantized* GGUF base is permitted by llama.cpp generally, but LoRA trained on bf16/NF4 weights applied to Q4_K_M base incurs mismatch — **[I]/UNVERIFIED** (no source fetched); safest: merge into bf16 then quantize.
- **Unsloth GGUF:** `model.save_pretrained_gguf("dir", tokenizer, quantization_method="q4_k_m")` (also q8_0, f16) and `push_to_hub_gguf`; also `save_pretrained_merged`/`save_method="lora"`. Warning: wrong chat template/EOS at inference is the common failure. https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.5/fine-tune (undated).
- **MLX:** `mlx_lm.fuse --model <base> [--adapter-path adapters]` → `fused_model/`; its `--export-gguf` is documented as limited to Mistral/Mixtral/Llama-style fp16 → **not for Qwen3.5**. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ml-explore/mlx-lm/main/mlx_lm/LORA.md (undated). **[I]** Cross-path: MLX adapters (mlx key names) are not PEFT adapters; to ship GGUF from a Mac-trained adapter you would fuse to fp16 HF safetensors (`mlx_lm.fuse --dequantize`—flag existence UNVERIFIED) then `convert_hf_to_gguf.py` + `llama-quantize`. Training on CUDA with PEFT avoids the problem.
- **Template caveat [D]:** Unsloth Mar-5 update fixed GGUF chat template (tool calling) for Qwen3.5 — re-download quants/templates if old. https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.5

## 7. Thinking mode

- **[D]** Qwen3.5 small models (0.8B/2B/4B/9B) are documented by Unsloth as reasoning **disabled by default**; toggle with `--chat-template-kwargs '{"enable_thinking":true|false}'` (PowerShell escaping `"{\"enable_thinking\":false}"`). https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/qwen3.5 (undated). **Contradiction:** the official `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B` chat_template.jinja fetched today emits `<think>\n` (thinking ON) unless `enable_thinking` is false; Unsloth's GGUF/template may differ. Always pass the flag explicitly.
- **[D]** No `/think`/`/no_think` soft switch in the official template (see §2). Use the template kwarg only.
- **[D] llama-server budget.** `--reasoning-budget N` token budget (PR #20297, 2026-03-11) forces end-of-think when exceeded via sampler state machine; per-request reasoning budget works (build b9982, 2026-07-13, https://jasonvsthenoise.com/repowatch/2026-07-13-llama-cpp-reasoning-budget-per-request/); `--reasoning-effort` added Aug 2026; request/CLI precedence was inverted at one point; lftw guide (as of master 2026-08-30): https://www.lftw.dev/guides/llama-cpp-reasoning-budget/. Discussion https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/23351 reports request-body `chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking` deprecated in recent builds in favour of a reasoning flag — **exact current per-request toggle UNVERIFIED; check `tools/server/README.md` of your build.**
- **[I] LoRA trained thinking-off, used with thinking on:** not tested in any source found. Expect the adapter to push style but the model may emit `<think>` content in the old style or skip/shorten thinking; the final answer style may leak persona into reasoning. Mitigations: keep thinking OFF for wording (Nova's use case); if enabling for rare repair queries, test on a small set; or mix ~10–25% short think examples (Unsloth suggests ≥75% reasoning to *preserve* reasoning — for a persona LoRA only mixing is needed). Use budget cap (e.g., 128–256 tokens) and strip `reasoning_content` before TTS.
